# ADR 0001 Vision and learning priority

Date: 2026-10-10

Status: Accepted by explicit user instruction

## Context

The earlier PRD included commercial validation gates. The latest request prioritizes deep AI engineering learning and a reliable cloud portfolio.

## Decision

Preserve Jarvis as AI Business Executive with dynamic teams and voice plus dashboard. Use synthetic scenarios and engineering evaluation. Revenue and a paying pilot are not current requirements.

## Alternatives

Commercial-first validation was previously suggested; it is superseded for this phase.

## Consequences

This changes the ordering and success gates, not the product vision. Learning evidence and implementation evidence remain separate.
